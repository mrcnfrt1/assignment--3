Design Rationale

Design Rationale

For this proof-of-concept, I wanted to make a calm, museum-style layout with a central portrait surrounded by eight painting techniques that link to Smarthistory. I came across Tippy.js while checking bias.wiki and decided to use it for the small hover/focus hint because it feels clean and easy to set up, working together with the center preview card.

If I take this further later, I could add ways to filter by art period or region, include a few artist examples, or add short notes to make exploring each technique even more helpful.
